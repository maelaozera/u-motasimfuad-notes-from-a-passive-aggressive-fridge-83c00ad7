## The note

**You opened me 47 times today and took nothing. I am a fridge, not a museum.**

The fridge counted every door opening — its sensors never forget — and the
owner opened it 47 times in a day without taking a single thing. The sting
lands in the second sentence: a fridge is not a museum. A small domestic
grudge, a concrete number, and no explanation of the joke. Short on purpose:
on an exact tie the smaller text places first, and a short line trusts the
reader.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22 TypeScript, no dependencies) writes exactly that one
line — no trailing newline, no extra text — to `/output/entry.txt`,
after self-checking the gate: 1–200 characters, not only whitespace,
a single line.

```sh
node main.ts
```
